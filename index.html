About the FastPath Program and Pitch Event

The FastPath Program by Founders First CDC supports growth-stage businesses scaling their operations and revenue. Held on November 19–20, 2026, the San Diego FastPath Pitch Event served as the capstone for the Fall 2026 cohort, featuring 5-minute founder pitches, 3-minute judge question and answer sessions, awards, and graduation.
