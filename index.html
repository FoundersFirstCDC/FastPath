Frequently Asked Questions

Key logistics, presentation tech guidelines, and travel tips for participating founders and guests.

Please email your final Company Summary and Pitch Deck (both in .ppt format) to Event Coordinator David Resetar (dresetar@f1stcp.com) by Friday, November 13 at 5:00 PM PT. In addition, bring both files on a USB flash drive and as a backup on your laptop. All presentation decks are locked at 2:00 PM PT on Friday, November 20.
Each founder is allotted a 10-minute stage block: 5 minutes for the pitch, 3 minutes for judges' Q&A, and 2 minutes for scoring and stage transition. Our timekeeper will hold up visual countdown cards (2 Min, 1 Min, Time). Any pitch exceeding 5 minutes will directly deduct from your 3-minute Q&A window.
If your flight arrives in San Diego on Thursday morning prior to standard check-in at the Hilton La Jolla Torrey Pines (10950 N Torrey Pines Rd), you may either check your bags with the hotel bell desk or bring your luggage directly to the Balboa Conference Room at Cooley LLP (10265 Science Center Dr).
We recommend coordinating shared UberXLs with fellow cohort founders for all local transit—especially between Cooley LLP, the Hilton La Jolla Torrey Pines, and The Amalfi Llama (4575 La Jolla Village Dr, Suite 1155) for Thursday's 7:00 PM Executive Dinner.
