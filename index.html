San Diego FastPath Pitch Event

November 19–20, 2026 • Cooley LLP, Torrey Pines, San Diego, CA

The FastPath Program by Founders First CDC supports growth-stage businesses scaling their operations and revenue. Held on November 19–20, 2026 at Cooley LLP in Torrey Pines, San Diego, CA, this capstone event featured 5-minute founder pitches, 3-minute judge question and answer sessions, awards, and graduation.

Program Team

Jose “Jay” Fulgencio, Ph.D.

Program Development Manager, Programs
Founders First CDC • Chicago, IL (CT)
jfulgencio@foundersfirstcdc.org (773) 347-7927 foundersfirstcdc.org
Read Bio

Dr. Jay Fulgencio is a Chicago-based Program Development Manager at Founders First CDC (having previously held program roles at Founders First), where he focuses on program design, curriculum delivery, and founder education. He brings experience as an entrepreneurship educator, instructional designer, and business owner.

Program Development: Manages program operations and supports digital learning and acceleration initiatives, including Passport Premium and FastPath. Facilitates cohort onboarding and leads member success resource sessions.

Business and Consulting: Founder and President of Learngenix, an e-learning platform and authorized reseller for MindEdge and Cypher Learning. Consults U.S. and international companies and entrepreneurs.

Academia and Governance: Teaches at Roosevelt University using Open Educational Resources (OER). Serves as an Elected Member of the Illinois Education Association (IEA) Higher Educational Council, Treasurer of IEA Region 67, and a member of the Roosevelt Adjunct Faculty Association.

Education and Credentials: Holds a Ph.D. and M.S. from Oklahoma State University and a B.A. from Northeastern Illinois University. Certified Financial Education Instructor (NFEC), Certified Practitioner of the Entrepreneurial Mindset Profile (Eckerd College), Master Online Leader and Administrator (UIS), Digital Teaching and Learning Certified Expert (Blackboard Academy), and Moodle Certified Educator.

Honors and Publications: Named a Negocios Now “40 Under 40” honoree and a USASBE Teaching and Learning Scholar. Author of personal finance and startup eBooks (with SALT) and writer for Millennial Magazine, Mic, and H&R Block Blog Talk.
