Jade Cabrera

Read Bio

Jade Cabrera serves as Community Manager for Central San Diego at JPMorganChase, where she expands access to economic opportunity through financial education and strategic community partnerships in her hometown.

Jade built the foundation of her career in Washington, D.C., where she spent years at the intersection of technology, policy, and public service. Most recently, she served as Manager of Public Policy External Engagement at Amazon Web Services, where she created pathways for founders and entrepreneurs shaping the next generation of technology to engage directly with D.C. policymakers on the future of their industries.

Prior to AWS, she spent two years as a public servant in the Federal government, serving at both the White House and the U.S. Department of Commerce's Office of Public Engagement—connecting communities, industry leaders and coalitions to the decision-making tables that shape national policy.

Today, Jade channels that national-scale experience in coalition-building and stakeholder engagement into local impact, deepening JPMorganChase's presence and investment in San Diego's communities.

Participant Guide

Frequently asked questions.

Submission deadlines, pitch rules, and San Diego logistics for November 19–20, 2026.

Email your final Company Summary and Pitch Deck (both in .ppt format) to David Resetar (dresetar@f1stcp.com) by Friday, November 13 at 5:00 PM PT. Bring both files on a USB flash drive and on your laptop. All slide decks lock at 2:00 PM PT on Friday, November 20.
Each founder has a 10-minute stage block: 5 minutes pitch, 3 minutes judges' Q&A, and 2 minutes stage swap. Visual countdown cards are displayed at 2 minutes, 1 minute, and Time. Any pitch exceeding 5 minutes deducts directly from your 3-minute Q&A window.
Leave luggage with the bell desk at Hilton La Jolla Torrey Pines (10950 N Torrey Pines Rd) or bring it to the Balboa Conference Room at Cooley LLP (10265 Science Center Dr).
Coordinate shared UberXLs with fellow cohort founders between Cooley LLP, Hilton La Jolla Torrey Pines, and The Amalfi Llama (4575 La Jolla Village Dr, Suite 1155) for Thursday's 7:00 PM Executive Dinner.
