Claudio Burgin serves as Assistant Vice President and Business Access Advisor at U.S. Bank, where he helps small business owners identify financial challenges, build customized growth plans, and connect with capital, financial education, and community resources.

Read Bio

Jade Cabrera serves as Community Manager for Central San Diego at JPMorganChase, where she expands access to economic opportunity through financial education and strategic community partnerships in her hometown.

Jade built the foundation of her career in Washington, D.C., where she spent years at the intersection of technology, policy, and public service. Today, Jade channels that national-scale experience in coalition-building and stakeholder engagement into local impact, deepening JPMorganChase's presence and investment in San Diego's communities.

Featured Session

Southern California Fireside Chat

Thursday, November 19, 2026 • Hosted by Adam Riggs-Zeigen

Felena Hanson

Founder, Hera Hub
herahub.com

Felena is an entrepreneur, author, and passionate advocate for women in business. As the visionary behind Hera Hub, a spa-inspired coworking space and business accelerator, she has dedicated her career to empowering female founders with the resources, connections, and strategies they need to succeed. Since launching her first location in San Diego in 2011, Felena has worked toward an ambitious mission: supporting over 20,000 women-led ventures through mentorship, education, community, and acceleration.

Session Overview

In this intimate conversation, Felena shared lessons learned from pioneering a new approach to coworking and entrepreneurial support—demonstrating the power of identifying unmet needs, building meaningful communities, and turning a bold vision into a sustainable business.

Key Insights Shared:

  • Turning vision into opportunity: Identifying market gaps and transforming ideas into businesses.
  • Building a community-driven business: Creating networks that foster collaboration and long-term growth.
  • Scaling with purpose: Expanding while staying connected to original mission and values.
  • Overcoming challenges: Navigating uncertainty and building resilience.
  • Leveraging mentorship: Understanding how access to strategic support systems accelerates success.
